Privacy Policy

LitLootUpload · Effective November 11, 2026

LitLootUpload is a small desktop tool run by Lit-Loot. It uploads videos from the operator's own computer to the operator's own YouTube channels and schedules when they go public. This policy explains what information the tool touches and what happens to it.

Who uses this app

LitLootUpload is a private tool used only by the Lit-Loot owner to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

LitLootUpload uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and approve access, the app can:

  • Upload videos to the YouTube channel you choose, and set their title, description, tags, privacy status and scheduled publish time.
  • Read your channel's name, to confirm uploads go to the intended channel.
  • Read your playlists and add uploaded videos to a playlist you choose.

The app does not read your email, contacts, watch history, comments, or any other Google account data.

How information is stored

  • Your Google sign-in token is saved only in a file on your own computer, in the app's folder.
  • A local log records which video files were uploaded and their YouTube links, so nothing is posted twice.
  • No information is sent to Lit-Loot servers or any third party. The app talks only to Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

Lit-Loot does not sell, rent, or share any information obtained through the app. Data goes only to YouTube, as needed to upload and organize your videos.

Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Revoke the app's access anytime at Google Account → Third-party connections, or via Google security settings.
  • Delete the token and log files from the app's folder to remove all locally stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
